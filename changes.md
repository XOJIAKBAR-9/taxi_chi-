1. The User Experience (UX) Flow
   For the Passengers
   Passenger opens Trip History in their profile.
   They select the recent trip and click "Report Lost Item".
   A form asks for a description of the item (e.g., "Black backpack", "iPhone 13") and permission to share a contact number.
   The system opens a temporary "Support Ticket" and notifies the driver.
   For the Driver:
   The driver receives a push notification: "Did you find a [Black backpack] in your Chevrolet Cobalt?"
   The driver checks the car. They tap either "Yes, I have it" or "No, I couldn't find it."
